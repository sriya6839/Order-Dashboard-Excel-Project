📊 Order Dashboard – Overview

This Order Dashboard brings together the most important sales and order insights in one place. It is designed to make the data easy to explore, compare, and understand at a glance.

🧾 Quick Performance Snapshot
The top KPI cards highlight Total Revenue, Average Revenue, Total Orders, and Average Discount, giving an instant view of overall performance.
🎯 Interactive Filtering
The Day and Sale Date slicers allow users to explore the dashboard based on specific days or dates and see how the results change.
🛍️ Product Performance
Compare product-wise revenue with average revenue.
Identify the products generating the highest revenue.
Discover the most ordered products.
📈 Sales & Revenue Trends
Track the number of sales across different days.
Compare daily revenue performance.
Analyze the difference between Actual Sales and Net Sales to understand the effect of discounts.
🎨 Clean & Visual Design
The combination of terracotta, mustard, and teal accents gives the dashboard a modern look while keeping the charts easy to read.
💡 Business Insights at a Glance
Instead of going through rows of raw data, the dashboard turns the information into clear visual insights, helping users quickly identify sales patterns, popular products, and revenue trends.

Overall, this dashboard transforms raw order data into a simple and interactive view of business performance.
